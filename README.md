# World War z

##Introduction
**World War Z**
* This movie is all about the whole world experience virus outbreak known as Zombie apocalypse and the objective of the main character is how to survive and solve the outbreak.
this movie was created at june 20, 2013 directed by Marc Forster also distributed by Paramount pictures with a genre of this are Action, Horror, Sci-Fi (Science Fiction), Thriller, and Apocalyptic/Post-Apocalyptic Fiction. This movie never got a  sequel by Paramount Pictures in February 2019 due to a combination of high production costs, script issues, and a strict Chinese ban on zombie and supernatural content.*
